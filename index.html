Outpatient Care

Coordinated MDT consultations designed for speed, continuity, and specialist alignment.

Patients access medical review, physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, and diagnostics through a unified outpatient experience.

  • Integrated specialist referrals
  • Clear clinical handoffs
  • Faster follow-up coordination
Inpatient Recovery

Structured recovery environments for stroke, neuro, orthopaedic, and paediatric rehabilitation.

Cabins, wards, therapy teams, and monitored treatment journeys come together in a disciplined inpatient model.

  • Dedicated neuro and stroke support
  • Therapy-first care journeys
  • Operationally efficient facility design
Assistive Services

Devices, prosthetics, orthotics, pharmacy, and diagnostics packaged as a complete support layer.

Patients can move from assessment to intervention with fewer disconnected stops and clearer service continuity.

  • Custom prosthetics and orthotics
  • On-site diagnostics and pharmacy
  • Independent living support through Aware Shop
Community Reach

Care beyond the facility through domiciliary support, family education, and long-term engagement.

For patients unable to visit the centre consistently, CRP Mirpur extends support through home-based rehabilitation pathways.

  • Home-based services
  • Family-centered coaching
  • Longitudinal patient relationships
