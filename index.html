About CRP Mirpur

Excellence in Rehabilitation Care Since 2003

The CRP Mirpur Centre is making a significant impact, serving over 28,000 beneficiaries each year, with demand for our services continually growing.

Operating from a 13-story building (134,882 sq ft) with a basement on land leased from the Government of Bangladesh (GoB), our centre began its journey in 2003 with the generous support of the Kadoorie Charitable Foundation (KCF).

CRP Mirpur sub-centre is operating under a business/income generation model, with annual transactions exceeding 200 million BDT.
